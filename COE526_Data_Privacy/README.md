# COE 526 — Interactive Privacy Labs
 

## Content and scope
 

**These labs are a teaching aid, not legal advice.** Legal content was last reviewed in **August 2026**. Several
items age quickly and should be re-verified before reuse in a later term:

- the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision and its litigation history
- U.S. comprehensive state privacy laws, which continue to be enacted and amended
- Saudi PDPL implementing and transfer regulations
