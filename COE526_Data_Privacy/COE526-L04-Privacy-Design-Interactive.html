01 · Legal idea → engineering decision

GDPR Article 25

Article 25 calls for appropriate technical and organizational measures for data protection by design and by default. The choice depends on current technology, implementation cost, processing context, and risk to people.

By design

Build safeguards into the full life cycle. Translate goals such as minimization into requirements the system can enforce and teams can test.

By default

Without user action, collect, retain, expose, and process only the personal data necessary for the specific purpose.

The engineering test: Can the software constrain the risky action, and can the team produce evidence that the constraint works?

Quick check: which default fits Article 25?

A new budgeting app can import ten years of transaction history, but its spending summary needs only twelve months.

Policy alone

“Developers must not use production customer records in tests.”

A statement sets intent but cannot intercept a copied database.

Engineered control

Test environments accept only generated or de-identified records; the pipeline blocks production identifiers.

Automated test: inject a canary identifier and verify rejection.
