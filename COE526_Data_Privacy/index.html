COE 526 — Data Privacy Interactive lab pack
Classroom labs

Interactive privacy labs

Three self-contained browser labs used alongside the COE 526 lectures. Each one runs offline with no accounts, no tracking, and no data leaving the browser. All scenarios are fictional.

01

Privacy foundations and terminology

Anonymity, pseudonymity, unlinkability, undetectability, and unobservability, plus an attack lab and a claim stress test that forces an adversary and an evaluation plan.

 02

Privacy law

FIPPs, OECD principles, the HIPAA Privacy Rule, GDPR, and the Saudi PDPL, with a comparison matrix, transfer timeline, scenario lab, and a fourteen-question quiz.

Teaching aid, not legal advice. Legal content was last reviewed in August 2026. Adequacy decisions, state privacy statutes, and implementing regulations change; verify sources before reusing this material in a later term.
