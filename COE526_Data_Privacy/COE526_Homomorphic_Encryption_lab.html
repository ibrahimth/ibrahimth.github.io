Foundational Academic References

  1. Armknecht et al. (2015), A Guide to Fully Homomorphic Encryption (ePrint 2015/1192)
  2. Rivest, Shamir & Adleman (1978), A Method for Obtaining Digital Signatures and Public-Key Cryptosystems
  3. ElGamal (1985), A Public Key Cryptosystem and a Signature Scheme Based on Discrete Logarithms
  4. Paillier (EUROCRYPT 1999), Public-Key Cryptosystems Based on Composite Degree Residuosity Classes
  5. Gentry (2009), A Fully Homomorphic Encryption Scheme (Stanford PhD Dissertation)
  6. Brakerski, Gentry and Vaikuntanathan (2012), (Leveled) Fully Homomorphic Encryption without Bootstrapping
  7. Fan & Vercauteren (2012), Somewhat Practical Fully Homomorphic Encryption (ePrint 2012/144)
  8. Cheon, Kim, Kim & Song (ASIACRYPT 2017), Homomorphic Encryption for Arithmetic of Approximate Numbers (ePrint 2016/421)
  9. Chillotti, Gama, Georgieva & Izabachene (2016), Faster FHE: Bootstrapping in Less Than 0.1 Seconds (ePrint 2016/870)
  10. Dwork & Roth (2014), The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy
  11. Microsoft SEAL — Official C++ Homomorphic Encryption Library
  12. OpenFHE — Open-Source Fully Homomorphic Encryption Library & Documentation
  13. HElib — Homomorphic Encryption Library (BGV / CKKS)
  14. HomomorphicEncryption.org, security guidelines and parameter resources
